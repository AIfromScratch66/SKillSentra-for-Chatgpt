# Data Asset Practitioner

Use this Skill for structured research and content involving Chinese data assets and data elements.

## Method

1. Fix the source scope and requested output.
2. Keep policy facts, research findings and recommendations distinct.
3. Maintain a source-to-claim ledger for material statements.
4. Report unsupported or unverified claims as unknown.

## Safety

Do not conflate registration, ownership, accounting recognition or valuation conclusions.
